Track Information

Monza, officially known as the Autodromo Nazionale Monza, is a historic motor racing circuit located near Milan in Italy. Opened in 1922, it is one of the oldest permanent racing circuits in the world and is best known as the home of the Italian Grand Prix. The track is famous for its extremely high speeds, long straights, and heavy braking zones. Monza is often called the “Temple of Speed” because of its fast layout and long motorsport history. Iconic sections of the circuit include the Rettifilo chicane, the Curva Grande, the two Lesmo corners, Ascari, and the famous Parabolica, now officially named Curva Alboreto. Over the years, Monza has produced many memorable Formula One races and dramatic championship moments. Today, it remains one of the most popular and important circuits in world motorsport.
