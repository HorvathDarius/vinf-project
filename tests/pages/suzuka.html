Track Information

Suzuka Circuit is a famous motor racing circuit located in Suzuka, Japan. It opened in 1962 and was originally built as a test track for Honda. Today, it is best known as the home of the Japanese Grand Prix and is one of the most respected circuits in world motorsport. Suzuka is famous for its unique figure-eight layout, where one part of the track crosses over another using a bridge. The circuit includes many challenging corners, such as the Esses, Degner, Spoon Curve, and the very fast 130R. Drivers often praise Suzuka because it requires precision, rhythm, bravery, and excellent car balance. Over the years, it has hosted many important Formula One races and championship-deciding moments. Today, Suzuka remains a favorite among drivers and fans because of its history, difficulty, and exciting racing.
