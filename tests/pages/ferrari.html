Team Profile

The Ferrari Formula 1 team, officially known as Scuderia Ferrari, is the sport’s most iconic and successful outfit, with a legacy stretching back over seven decades. Founded in 1929 by Enzo Ferrari, it initially served as a racing division for Alfa Romeo but began designing and building its own cars after World War II, entering the Formula One World Championship in the very first season in 1950. From the start, Ferrari established itself as a formidable force, winning its first F1 World Championship with Alberto Ascari in 1952 and quickly becoming synonymous with motorsport passion and technical innovation.
