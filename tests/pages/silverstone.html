Track Information

Silverstone Circuit is one of the most famous motor racing venues in the world and is best known as the home of the British Grand Prix. Located in Northamptonshire, England, the circuit was built on a former World War II airfield and hosted the first Formula One World Championship race in 1950. Silverstone is known for its fast, flowing layout and iconic corners such as Copse, Maggotts, Becketts, and Stowe. Today, it hosts major motorsport events including Formula One, MotoGP, endurance racing, and classic car festivals.
